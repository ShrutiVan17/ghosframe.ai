# 90-second Demo Script

## Opening
“Fake and AI-assisted movie trailers increasingly look like official studio releases. GhostFrame verifies the claim being made about media, not simply whether AI was used.”

## Demo
1. Enter: **This is the official trailer for [movie].**
2. Click **Investigate Claim**.
3. Point out that Gemini decomposes the claim into verification questions.
4. Show Parallel retrieving live evidence.
5. Show official/reputable/web source tiers.
6. Show the deterministic support vs contradiction score.
7. Reveal the verdict and Gemini-grounded explanation.

## Closing
“GhostFrame turns a confusing trailer into an auditable evidence trail: what was claimed, what the web actually supports, where the contradictions are, and how confident we should be.”
